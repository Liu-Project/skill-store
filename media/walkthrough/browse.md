## One marketplace for every Agent Skill

SkillStore reads any registry that implements the open REST contract. Point it at a local registry, your team server, or a public index.

- **Card view** — search, filter, and sort skills in a full editor tab.
- **Sidebar** — keep the marketplace next to your files.
- **Multi-registry** — switch between registries without reconfiguring anything.

Run **SkillStore: Card View** from the Command Palette to open the marketplace.
